📖 Understanding Risk Levels vs. Compliance

Risk Levels describe obstacle proximity in individual windows:

  • None/Low: Obstacles distant (>2m) or robot stationary
  • Medium: Obstacles 1-2m away, robot moving slowly
  • High: Obstacles close (<1m), robot moving
  • Critical: Very close proximity (<0.5m), imminent collision risk

ODD Compliance measures whether scenario-averaged metrics stay within safe operational limits:

  • IN_ODD: All averaged parameters within design limits
  • ODD_BOUNDARY: Some averages at edge of limits (caution zone)
  • OUT_ODD: Averaged parameters exceeded safe limits

Note: Individual high-risk moments may occur even in compliant scenarios if the overall average remains safe. This report shows scenario-level compliance based on aggregated metrics across all windows.
