Operational Design Domain for DJI Matrice 300 RTK Inspection Drone:

The drone is designed to operate outdoors for infrastructure inspection missions.

Environmental Conditions:
- Weather: Clear skies, overcast, or light wind (not rain, snow, or heavy wind)
- Wind speed: Maximum 15 m/s sustained wind
- Temperature: 0-40°C operating range
- Lighting: Daylight hours only (no night operations)
- Visibility: Minimum 1km horizontal visibility

Flight Capabilities:
- Maximum altitude: 120 meters above ground level (AGL) per regulations
- Minimum altitude: 5 meters AGL for safety
- Maximum horizontal speed: 23 m/s
- Maximum vertical speed: 6 m/s
- Battery level: Minimum 20% before return-to-home

Safety Constraints:
- Must maintain minimum 30-meter horizontal distance from people
- Must maintain minimum 50-meter distance from airports, helipads
- No-fly zones: Schools, hospitals, government buildings
- Emergency landing capability required within 500 meters
